# Experiment: div-impl

Combinational versus iterative DIV: the ALU's single-cycle divider against vcpu8_divider, a restoring divider that finds one quotient bit per clock (8 iterations). It measures what the 8 extra cycles per DIV cost against the area and the Fmax the iterative divider saves.

Revision `vcpu8-mc-r1`, ISA 1.0.0, commit `6028b9883014880868c176d1b036f6f061f802d8`, 2026-10-17T02:48:44.680Z. Toolchain: OSS CAD Suite 2026-10-16.

Each variant ran `verify` (fixtures, examples, programs, regressions, random; 1000 random programs from seed 1), then the benchmark (20 programs in `examples`), then `make synth`. A variant that fails a stage gets no metrics. Deltas are against `div-comb`. Cycles are RTL clock edges; Fmax is nextpnr's estimate for the named device, and suite time is the suite's cycles / that Fmax; cells are generic Yosys cells, not transistors; logic depth is the gates on the longest path of the generic netlist, not a delay.
