Correctness gate: **PASSED**, 20/20 programs. Each program runs on @vcpu/cpu-core (live) and on the RTL runner; every trace step and the full end state (registers, flags, PC, SP, SD, status, fault, all 256 memory bytes) must match, and the program must halt. A program that fails gets no numbers.

- RTL: clock cycles counted at real clock edges of the Verilated vcpu8_top, from reset release to the stop.
- Instructional model: the emulator’s teaching estimate (@vcpu/cpu-core estimatedCycles, the ISA timing model). It is not a circuit and was not measured.
- Estimated time: RTL cycles / nextpnr Fmax for the named device and timing model, at the place-and-route seed in the synthesis summary. A static timing estimate, not a measurement on hardware.
