# Experiment: smoke

Smoke test of the experiment framework: the committed design as its only variant, so every stage (runner build, verify, benchmark, synth, comparison) runs once and its numbers must equal the committed baseline's.

Revision `vcpu8-mc-r1`, ISA 1.0.0, commit `462082293956bd3c347943b58a55965ceea374d9`, 2026-10-17T02:14:17.475Z. Toolchain: OSS CAD Suite 2026-10-16.

Each variant ran `verify` (fixtures, examples, programs, regressions, random; 200 random programs from seed 1), then the benchmark (20 programs in `examples`), then `make synth`. A variant that fails a stage gets no metrics. Deltas are against `default`. Cycles are RTL clock edges; Fmax is nextpnr's estimate for the named device; cells are generic Yosys cells, not transistors.
