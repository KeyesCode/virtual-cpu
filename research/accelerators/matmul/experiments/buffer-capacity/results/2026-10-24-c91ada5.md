# Experiment: buffer-capacity

MAX_DIM 32 versus 64 versus 128 on the 8 × 8 array: the cells and memory bits each buffer capacity costs against the largest workload it can run.

Experiment ID `buffer-capacity/2026-10-24-c91ada5`. Revision `matmul-os-r1`, commit `c91ada5`. Every variant passed every stage.

Each variant was built, then verified against `@vcpu/accelerator-reference` (the directed corpus and 40 random cases from seed 1), then benchmarked (operand seed 1, every case gated on the reference), then synthesised with every flow of `make synth`. A variant that fails a stage has no metrics. Deltas are against the baseline, `max-dim-64`.

- Cycles: rising clock edges of the Verilated matmul_top, counted by the Verilator runner (tb/sim/matmul_sim.cpp). They are clocks, not time: no clock frequency is assumed, so no time or throughput per second is reported.
- One MAC is one multiply plus one accumulate: a signed INT8 × INT8 product added into a signed INT32 accumulator. A workload of M × K by K × N is M · N · K MACs. Ops count each MAC as two operations (ops = 2 × MACs).
- PE utilization = MACs / (ROWS · COLS · compute cycles): the fraction of PE-clocks of the compute phase (PREPARE_TILE, COMPUTE and DRAIN of every tile) that did a useful MAC. STORE, transfer and handshake clocks are not in the denominator.
- Synthesis: generic cells are Yosys internal cells, FPGA counts are what Yosys mapped the design to, and Fmax is nextpnr's estimate for a named device, all with the identical settings of `make synth` (docs/hardware/accelerators/synthesis-guide.md).
- Power: not measured. No variant is ranked on power.

## Pareto-optimal variants

Axes: (1) total cycles summed over the common workloads and (2) generic cells; lower is better on both. A variant is Pareto-optimal when no other variant has at most its total cycles and at most its generic cells, and fewer of one. Total cycles are summed over the workloads every passed variant ran; failed variants are left out.

Common workloads (5): `4x4x4`, `8x8x8`, `16x16x16`, `32x32x32`, `13x19x11`. Pareto-optimal: **max-dim-32**.
