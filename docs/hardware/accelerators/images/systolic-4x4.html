4 × 4 output-stationary systolic array, clock by clock

One tile of C = A · B with A 4 × 3 and B 3 × 4 (K = 3), signed INT8 operands. PE(i, j) keeps C[i, j] in its accumulator; A moves right and B moves down. The caller presents column k of A and row k of B unskewed in clock k + 1; row i passes i skew registers and column j passes j, so PE(i, j) multiplies A[i, k] × B[k, j] in clock k + i + j + 1, and tile_done pulses in clock K + ROWS + COLS − 1 = 10. Clocks are counted from the tile_start clock, 0, exactly as in systolic-array.md.

How to read it

  • Each PE shows its name and, when it works this clock, the k of its pair (bold outline, green): the A × B operands it multiplies, its accumulator register in this clock, and the product added at the end of the clock with the value it leaves (+= p → v). Dashed outline: idle. Grey with ✓: the accumulator already holds C[i, j].
  • The boxes left of the grid are row i's i skew registers, above it column j's j: each shows the operand it holds this clock. Clock 0 is tile_start (the accumulators are cleared at its end), clocks 1 … 3 are the operand clocks, and from clock 4 the array only drains what is already inside it.
